Who we are

We are HIJAZI Apartments (شقق حجازي المفروشة). We rent fully furnished apartments in Shmeisani, Amman, next to Abdali. We offer a stay that is more spacious and private than a hotel room, with comfort, elegance and free regular housekeeping, so you feel at home whether you come with your family, for work or to accompany a patient.

Every apartment listed on the website is 220 m² with 3 bedrooms (one master), 3 bathrooms, a separate living room, a guest room, a fully equipped kitchen and a laundry room, and sleeps 7–8 guests. The location is close to restaurants, cafés, supermarkets and daily services.

Location: Shmeisani, next to Abdali

Our apartments are in Shmeisani, Amman, next to Abdali. The location is close to Jordan Hospital and the Specialty Hospital in Shmeisani, and to the Islamic Hospital and Abdali Hospital in Abdali, as well as restaurants, cafés, supermarkets and daily services.

Time to the hospitals (Google Maps)

  • The Specialty Hospital: an 8-minute walk (650 m), or a 3-minute drive
  • The Islamic Hospital: a 6-minute drive (2.3 km), or a 23-minute walk
  • Jordan Hospital: a 7-minute drive (3.4 km), or a 29-minute walk
  • Abdali Hospital: a 7-minute drive (3.1 km), or a 29-minute walk

Within walking distance

  • Jawharat Al Shmeisani Pharmacy and Taif Pharmacy: 4–5 minutes
  • Dunya Haya grocery: 5 minutes, and Ashour Al Shmisani Supermarket: 6 minutes
  • The Nub, Vinaigrette, Trattoria and Peking restaurants: 4–5 minutes
  • The Qyard and GEMINI Terrace cafés: 5 minutes

Approximate times from Google Maps (November 2026); driving time depends on traffic, and shop names can change.

To confirm a hospital's opening hours and address, please contact the hospital directly.
